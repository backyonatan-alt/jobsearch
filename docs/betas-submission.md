**Short description** (583/600)
```
Most interview prep is ChatGPT plus an hour of googling — and it often lands on the wrong same-named company, with claims you can't back up when the interviewer asks where you read that.

Pursuit researches the real company and your actual interviewer, round by round, with a source link behind every claim. Add your CV and the brief tells you what to make sure they hear. It also tracks your applications, so round 2's prep knows what round 1 asked.

Ready to try now: sign in with Google, add a role and an interviewer, get a playbook in about a minute. 10 free playbooks, no card.
```
